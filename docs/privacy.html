Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dark Media Engine handles information when an authorized operator connects social accounts and uses supported content-management features.

1. Information we process

When you connect a supported social account, the service may process basic account information made available through the platform’s official API, such as an account identifier, display name, profile information, granted scopes, authorization tokens and token expiration data.

The service may also process content you choose to prepare or distribute, including titles, captions, scripts, video files, publication status and provider responses needed to operate the requested workflow.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested functionality, such as identifying the connected account, maintaining authorization, preparing content, uploading approved media, tracking delivery status and diagnosing integration errors.

3. Authentication and passwords

Dark Media Engine uses official OAuth or authorization flows offered by connected platforms. The service does not request or store your TikTok or Google account password.

4. Token storage and security

Authorization tokens are intended to remain backend-only and may be encrypted before local persistence. Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with a connected platform only when required to perform the action you requested, such as authenticating an account or uploading content through that platform’s official API.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the operation of the service, troubleshooting, or compliance. Connected-account authorization may be revoked through the applicable platform. Local development data may also be deleted by the operator managing the installation.

7. Third-party platforms

Your use of TikTok, YouTube, Google and other connected services is also governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Dark Media Engine does not control those third-party services.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service and integrations evolve. The effective date above identifies the current version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the project owner’s public GitHub profile: https://github.com/haillfear-dev.
